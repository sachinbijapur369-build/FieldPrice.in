Counterfeit Awareness

How to spot a counterfeit bearing before it damages your machine.

Counterfeit bearings are a serious problem in the Indian market. A fake bearing can fail prematurely, damage expensive equipment and create safety hazards. Here are the warning signs.

Warning signs of counterfeit bearings

  1. Price too low: If the price is more than 40% below the authorised market rate for the brand, question the source.
  2. Poor packaging: Spelling errors, low-quality printing, missing hologram stickers or incorrect brand colours on the box.
  3. Rough finish: Genuine bearings have smooth, consistent grinding marks. Counterfeits often show rough surfaces, tool marks or uneven finishes.
  4. Misaligned laser engraving: Check the bearing markings — genuine parts have crisp, evenly spaced laser-engraved characters.
  5. No authorised distributor invoice: If the seller cannot provide an invoice from an authorised distributor chain, the supply is unverified.
