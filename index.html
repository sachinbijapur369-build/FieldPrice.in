The Problem

Industrial MRO pricing has no transparency.

We spent years on the distribution side. We know exactly how margins are applied, when quotes are inflated, and which buyers are consistently overcharged. That knowledge now works for the buyer.

Real example: A 210L drum of Shell Tellus S2 MX 68 quoted at ₹32,000 by one distributor and ₹24,500 by another — same brand, same grade, same week. The buyer who checked saved ₹7,500 per drum.
Real example: A set of SKF 6205-2RS bearings priced at ₹700 through an authorised channel vs ₹800 through a general dealer. Same genuine product, 12.5% price difference.
01 —

No price reference exists

There is no public index for industrial lubricant, belt or bearing pricing in India. Buyers accept whatever they are quoted, with no benchmark to validate it.

02 —

Large buyers often pay more, not less

Procurement is delegated to staff who do not question prices. Distributors recognise this and price accordingly. Volume alone does not earn a better rate.

03 —

The same product can vary 25–40%

Identical product, identical brand, same week — quoted at dramatically different prices to different buyers. Awareness is the only variable that changes the outcome.

What Buyers Typically Ask

Straightforward answers for industrial teams.

We hear the same questions from maintenance heads, procurement managers and plant engineers. Here are the honest answers.

Is this really free? Yes. There is no fee for receiving a verified quote. We earn when you choose to source through us — our margin comes from the distributor side, not from you.
What if I already have a supplier? Use our benchmark to negotiate. Many buyers use FieldPrice quotes as a second reference to validate what their current supplier charges.
Do you deliver or just quote? We facilitate the full sourcing — from specification verification through to delivery from the authorised distributor to your plant.
What's the minimum order? There is no minimum. We verify quotes for a single bearing, a single drum of oil or a full annual rate contract.
Can you handle annual rate contracts? Yes. We can benchmark annual pricing, verify rates quarterly, and flag when distributor pricing shifts during the contract period.
How is this different from IndiaMART or TradeIndia? Those are listing platforms. We are sales engineers who verify specification, confirm authorised supply and benchmark the real market rate before sending you a price.
