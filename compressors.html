Sizing tip

Always size compressors based on actual CFM demand plus 20–25% safety margin. Oversizing wastes energy, undersizing causes pressure drops and production issues. A compressed air audit can identify the optimal size for your facility.

Buyer's Checklist

Know these six things before buying an air compressor.

Getting the scope right upfront leads to faster quotes, fewer surprises and a lower total cost of ownership. Check these before sending your requirement.

  • Actual air demand (CFM) — Calculate total CFM from all connected pneumatic tools, machines and processes. Add 20–25% safety margin.
  • Required pressure (bar/psi) — Check the minimum inlet pressure needed by your most pressure-sensitive equipment.
  • Duty cycle — Continuous (factory air) or intermittent (workshop use). This determines screw vs reciprocating.
  • Air quality class — General industrial, instrument-grade, or Class 0 oil-free. This dramatically affects compressor type and price.
  • Installation scope — Confirm whether dryer, receiver tank, piping, electrical wiring, installation and commissioning are included in the quote.
  • Service availability — Authorised service presence at your location, spare parts lead time and AMC response commitment.
